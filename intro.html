Where it started · c. 2500 BCE · Indus Valley · today Pakistan & India
The Great Bath of Mohenjo-daro
Among the oldest public bathing places on Earth
A brick pool sealed with bitumen, read as a place of ritual purification. About 2,000 years before the Greek healing temples.
India · from c. 1500 BCE
Ayurveda & Yoga
“The science of life” — balance of body, mind and breath
Ayurveda: herbs, diet, massage and cleansing, written down in the Charaka and Sushruta Samhitas. Patanjali's Yoga Sutras join body, breath and mind.
China · c. 3rd–1st c. BCE
Huangdi Neijing
Qi, yin and yang, and the art of acupuncture
The Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic: life energy (qi) flows through meridians. Base of Traditional Chinese Medicine; acupuncture is UNESCO heritage since 2010.
Japan · Onsen
Dōgo Onsen
Three thousand years of healing waters
Mentioned in Japan's oldest chronicle (720 CE). In the Edo period people travelled for tōji, weeks-long stays at hot springs to heal.
Thailand · UNESCO Heritage 2019
Nuad Thai
Traditional Thai massage, a living heritage of touch
Traced to Jivaka, physician of the Buddha. In the 1830s King Rama III had its knowledge carved in stone at Wat Pho temple in Bangkok.
Today
Asia — the world’s medical & wellness hub
Thailand · India · Singapore · Malaysia · Korea
After the 1997 crisis hospitals like Bumrungrad turned to foreign patients. In 2024 Asia-Pacific became the largest wellness tourism region: about 560 million trips.
